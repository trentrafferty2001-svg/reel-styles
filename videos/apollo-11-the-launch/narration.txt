[calm, documentary narrator, unhurried] July sixteenth, nineteen sixty-nine. A hot morning in Florida. [short pause] The Saturn Five stood taller than a thirty-story building, and most of its weight was fuel. Nearly a million people lined the beaches and roads to watch. At nine thirty-two, five engines lit, and the ground shook for miles. In under three minutes, it burned two thousand tonnes of fuel and climbed sixty kilometres. [softer] Four days later, two of the men aboard walked on the Moon. Follow for more.
