# How compound interest works (reel-cartoon)

Mascot: Coinny, an original round gold coin (gold #ffc83d, ink outline, white eyes, small arms/legs).
Background: sky #5ec8ff with soft gradient, grass hill #3ec46d at the bottom.
Maths: $1,000 at 8% a year for 40 years = $21,725 compounded; $4,200 simple interest. Year one interest $80.

Scene 1 (hook): "One thousand dollars. Leave it alone for forty years, and it turns into twenty-one thousand. How?"
  Giant "$1,000" slams in with a shake (thud); a "40 YEARS" stamp; the number rolls up to "$21,725" (tick); Coinny shocked, squash.
Scene 2: "Say it earns eight percent a year. Year one, you get eighty dollars."
  Whip pan in. An "8% / year" sign drops (pop); a small "$80" coin pops out of Coinny (boing). Coinny happy.
Scene 3: "Here's the trick. Next year, that eighty earns interest too. Your money makes baby money."
  Thought-bubble style: the $80 baby coin sprouts its own tiny coin (pop); baby coins wear little bonnets/blink. Coinny smug.
Scene 4: "And the babies make babies. A bigger pile grows faster every year."
  Coin pile multiplies in waves (pop, pop, pop), a curve line climbs bending upward. Coinny dizzy, eyes tracking up.
Scene 5: "Without compounding, you'd end with about four thousand. With it, over twenty-one thousand."
  Two bars: grey "Simple $4,200" short, green "Compound $21,725" shoots tall (whoosh). Coinny shocked then happy.
Scene 6 (payoff + end): "The secret ingredient is time. Start early. Follow for more."
  Hourglass + snowball rolling; big number lands "$21,725" with confetti (ding); end card: Coinny waving, "Follow for more".
